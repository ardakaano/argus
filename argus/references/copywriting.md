# Copy pack guidance

All copy is a **draft**. Write it in the user's market language. Never invent testimonials, numbers, customers or awards. Use only claims the product can back up today.

## Positioning sentence

`For [primary ICP] who [trigger or job], [Product] is a [category] that [key benefit], unlike [main alternative] which [limitation].`

If a claim cannot be defended, soften it or drop it.

## Three hooks

Use three **different angles**, not three rewordings of the same line:

1. **Pain**: names the problem in the customer's own words.
2. **Outcome**: describes the result after using it.
3. **Contrast**: states the trade-off against the alternative.

## Launch post

- Open with who it's for and the problem. Product name comes after.
- Say what exists today and what is rough. Honesty gets better feedback than polish.
- Make one specific ask (try it, share a use case, tell me what's missing).
- No hype words, fake urgency or emoji spam.
- Fit the venue: a Show-and-tell thread, a launch directory and a founders' community each have different norms.

## Community-safe reply

- Answer the person's actual question first, fully, without needing the product.
- Mention the product only if it's relevant and the rules allow it. Disclose that you made it.
- Don't paste the same reply twice. Don't reply to posts older than the venue considers fresh.
- No DMs to people who did not ask.

## Before the user posts anything

Remind them to re-read the venue's current rules, post from their own account, and approve the final text. Argus drafts and never posts.
