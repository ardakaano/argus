# Argus — FormNest

**Mode:** hypothesis-only  
**Market:** English-speaking solo SaaS founders

> ⚠️ Nothing in this report is verified. Treat every claim as a hypothesis and check it before acting.

## Verdict
Hypothesis-only demo: validate whether solo SaaS founders want a lightweight in-app feedback widget before spending anything on distribution. Start with participation-first founder communities, because they are free and give feedback within days.

**Biggest uncertainty:** Founders may already be 'good enough' with a free form or a shared inbox, which would make the pain real but unpaid.

## Product snapshot
- **Problem:** Small SaaS teams hear about problems late, because feedback tools are heavy to set up.
- **Promise:** Collect meaningful in-app feedback in minutes, without a heavyweight tool.
- **Stage:** Private beta
- **Pricing:** Not set
- **Call to action:** Join the beta
- **Differentiation:** Setup time measured in minutes, not days.

## Fixes on your own pages
- The page promises 'minutes' but shows no setup walkthrough; add a 60-second demo.
- Pricing is 'Not set'; even a placeholder range would help beta users decide.

## Ideal customers

### Solo SaaS founder with 50-500 users (primary)
- **Job to be done:** Learn why users churn or get stuck without running surveys by hand
- **Trigger:** A churn spike or a support thread that reveals a missing feature
- **Alternatives:** Email, Typeform, a shared inbox, a free feedback board
- **Objections:** One more script on my site; I can ask users by email

### Product-minded developer at a 3-10 person startup (secondary)
- **Job to be done:** Give the team a lightweight channel for user voice
- **Trigger:** First hire in support or product
- **Alternatives:** Intercom, Canny, internal Slack channel
- **Objections:** We already pay for a support tool

## 30-day sprint

### Week 1 — Confirm the pain and the promise
- Hold five short conversations with target founders
- Draft two landing-page promises
- **Signal:** At least 3 of 5 describe the pain without being prompted
- **Stop/go gate:** Fewer than 2 confirm: change the ICP before building more

### Week 2 — Test the top two channels
- Share one useful post where allowed
- Run five opt-in demos
- **Signal:** Qualified replies or demo requests
- **Stop/go gate:** No qualified replies after a fair attempt: change the message, not the volume

### Week 3 — Checkpoint, then activation
- Simplify installation based on demo friction
- Follow up with consenting testers
- **Signal:** Testers reach their first feedback
- **Stop/go gate:** Add channel #3 only if one channel produced signal

### Week 4 — Decide
- Compare activation by channel
- Stop weak experiments
- **Signal:** One channel clearly outperforms
- **Stop/go gate:** No channel has signal: pause and revisit the ICP

## Copy drafts
**Positioning:** For solo SaaS founders who learn about problems too late, FormNest is a lightweight feedback widget that installs in minutes, unlike heavy tools that need a sprint to set up.

**Hooks**
- You find out why users left three weeks after they left.
- Hear from users inside your app, with five minutes of setup.
- Not another feedback suite: one widget, one inbox.

**Launch post**

> I built FormNest, a small feedback widget for solo SaaS apps.
> It's in private beta and still rough around onboarding.
> I'm looking for 5 founders who'd try it and tell me what's missing.

**Reply draft**

> The trade-off with feedback tools is usually setup time versus workflow depth. What matters most for your app right now? (Disclosure: I'm building a small tool in this space.)

**Outreach draft (one to one)**

> Hi Sam, I'm building a small feedback widget for SaaS apps and you're the first person I thought of: you ship a product solo. Could I ask you 3 questions about how you hear from users today? 10 minutes, no pitch, and a plain 'no thanks' is totally fine.

## Experiments
1. **Target founders describe late feedback as a real pain without prompting**
   - Channel: Direct conversations
   - Test: Five 15-minute calls with founders found in communities where they already talk about the topic
   - KPI: Calls where the pain is raised unprompted
   - Gate: Proposed: 3 of 5 continue, fewer than 2 means change the ICP
   - ⏱ can start within 48h
2. **Promise A ('know why users leave') beats promise B ('five-minute setup')**
   - Channel: Landing page
   - Test: Two headlines shown to opt-in beta visitors
   - KPI: Beta sign-up rate per headline
   - Gate: Proposed: review after 100 visitors per variant; a clear gap decides
3. **A useful founder-community post produces qualified conversations**
   - Channel: Founder communities
   - Test: One educational post where allowed, then reply to every comment
   - KPI: Qualified conversations per post
   - Gate: Proposed: 3 qualified conversations continues the channel
4. **Shorter setup increases activation**
   - Channel: Beta onboarding
   - Test: Compare the current onboarding with a concierge setup for opt-in testers
   - KPI: Install to first feedback rate
   - Gate: Proposed: review after 15 qualified installs
5. **Founders will commit to pay before the product is finished**
   - Channel: Beta testers
   - Test: Offer a discounted founding-member price to active testers
   - KPI: Testers who commit
   - Gate: Proposed: 2 of 10 active testers commit
   - Budget ceiling: $0
